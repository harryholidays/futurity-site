Futurity & MetGen sign lignin focused technology licence for New Zealand

We’re happy to announce a new license agreement with Futurity Bio-Ventures. New Zealand-based company shares our mission in maximising the value of biomass and replacing petrochemicals in our everyday lives with renewable alternatives.Through this agreement, Futurity can build and operate lignin-centric biorefineries using METNIN® Technologies.The best part of the collaboration is that we aim for products that are better and more affordable than the old-fashioned fossils. We’re looking forward to changing the world together – Congratulations to New Zealand for being pioneers for future tech!
